About the fair

Research is better when it’s shared.

Research makes an impact when people hear about it.

A research poster turns a complex project into a visual story that people can understand, question, discuss and link to new ideas.

At the IndabaX Nigeria Poster Fair 2026, researchers and students will bring their work out of papers and presentations and into conversation with a wider community. Sometimes one question after a poster presentation is all it takes to start a new collaboration.

Who should attend

A community for every curiosity.

The fair is open to a diverse research and innovation community. You do not need to work in the same field as everyone else.

  • 01Students and early-career researchers
  • 02Academics and research professionals
  • 03AI and machine learning enthusiasts
  • 04Data scientists and computational researchers
  • 05Innovators and technology practitioners
  • 06Anyone interested in learning from emerging research

Interdisciplinary conversations are often where new ideas begin.
